Loquita: the duet that went to No.1

Claydee wrote, sang and produced the 2019 summer hit with Eleni Foureira. It topped the Greek chart and won Best Duet.

2019

Listen on Spotify Watch on YouTube

"Loquita" came out on 5 July 2019 and quickly became one of the songs of that Greek summer. Sung in Spanish by Eleni Foureira and Claydee, it pairs Latin pop with the club production Claydee had been refining since "Mamacita Buena".

Claydee is on every side of the record: he co-wrote it, sings on it, and produced it with Ted Economou. The single went to No.1 on the Greek digital singles chart, and the following year it won Best Duet at the MAD Video Music Awards.
