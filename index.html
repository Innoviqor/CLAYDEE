Where the hits get made

Claydee's Studio is a private production room in Alimos, in the southern suburbs of Athens. It's where Claydee writes, records and finishes his own records and the songs he makes for other artists.

You work directly with the people making the record: Claydee and the producers, writers and session players he brings in for each project. Dance, pop and Latin are home ground, from radio singles to club records.

Meet Claydee

Claydee (Klejdi Lupa) was born in Tirana and grew up in Athens, where his family moved when he was four. He trained as an audio engineer at SAE and took a BA in Recording Arts from Middlesex University before going on to work at MTV Greece.

His 2012 single "Mamacita Buena" went to No.1 on Greek airplay, came out internationally on Ultra Music and ended up in NBC's Telenovela. "Sexy Papi" followed it to No.1 in Greece, Poland and Serbia, and he was nominated for Best Greek Act at the MTV EMAs.

Behind the desk he has written and produced for Sakis Rouvas, Tamta, Kostas Martakis and Nikos Ganos, and runs two side projects with their own global hits: the masked duo Bang La Decks and The Prince Karma. In 2026 he co-wrote and co-produced "Jalla", Cyprus's Eurovision entry.
