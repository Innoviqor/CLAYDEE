Claydee co-wrote and co-produced Jalla, Cyprus's Eurovision song

Antigoni's high-energy entry took Cyprus to the final in Vienna, went Platinum in Greece and spun off three official remixes.

Eurovision 2026

Listen on Spotify Watch on YouTube

When Antigoni stepped onto the stage in Vienna for the 70th Eurovision Song Contest, the song she sang for Cyprus had been shaped in part by Claydee. Credited under his real name, Klejdi Lupa, he is one of the writers and one of the four producers of "Jalla", an up-tempo track that blends traditional Cypriot sounds with big, club-sized beats. In Cypriot dialect, jalla means "more".

From Athens to Vienna

The song premiered on 8 February 2026 on the main evening news of CyBC, the Cypriot public broadcaster, and was released the same day on Minos EMI, alongside a music video filmed across Cyprus. Antigoni performed it in the second half of the second semi-final on 14 May, qualified with 122 points and finished 19th in the final.

Its run continued well after the contest. Besides its Greek chart peak, "Jalla" reached No.5 on Sweden's Heatseeker chart and charted in Lithuania, Austria and the UK sales chart.

Remixes and new versions

After Eurovision, the song was reworked for different floors and audiences:

  • Afro-house rework with Gigi 21 May 2026
  • Arabic remix with Oualid 22 May 2026
  • Anastasia remix 2 July 2026

The Claydee sound

"Jalla" adds a Eurovision chapter to a catalogue that already includes "Loquita" with Eleni Foureira, Bang La Decks' "Utopia" on FIFA 15 and The Prince Karma's "Later Bitches". It's the same mix of Mediterranean flavour and dance-floor punch that artists come to the studio in Elliniko for.
