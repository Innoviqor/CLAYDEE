Writing for others

Before and alongside his own records, Claydee has written and produced for some of the biggest names in Greek pop, from Sakis Rouvas and Tamta to Eleni Foureira and Cyprus's 2026 Eurovision entry.

23 songs for other artists, 2008 to today. Tap a cover to play it on Spotify.

Claydee started out behind the desk. Working first under the alias Beetkraft and later under his own name, Klejdi Lupa, he wrote and produced for Greek pop and urban artists for years before his own breakthrough with "Mamacita Buena". He has kept doing it ever since, most recently on "Jalla", the song Antigoni sang for Cyprus at Eurovision 2026.
