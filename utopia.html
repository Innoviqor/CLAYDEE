Utopia: from FIFA 15 to Furious 7

A track by Claydee's masked duo Bang La Decks that found its way into a video game, a blockbuster soundtrack and TV.

Bang La Decks

Listen on Spotify Watch on YouTube

Bang La Decks is Claydee's masked EDM duo, a side project built for festival-sized drops. "Utopia", released through Ultra Music, became its best-known track when EA picked it for the FIFA 15 soundtrack.

The song then took on a second life. Aero Chord's Festival Trap Remix of "Utopia" was sampled on Sevyn Streeter's "How Bad Do You Want It (Oh Yeah)", released in February 2015 as a single from the Furious 7 soundtrack. "Utopia" has also been used in the TV series Power and Teen Wolf.
