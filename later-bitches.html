Later Bitches: from Athens to clubs worldwide

Released under Claydee's side project The Prince Karma, it started in Greece and ended up on airplay charts across Europe.

2018

Listen on Spotify Watch on YouTube

The Prince Karma is one of Claydee's side projects, a separate identity for straight-ahead club records. Its first single, "Later Bitches", came out on 30 March 2018 in Greece and Cyprus, and within a month it was trending on Shazam worldwide.

Ultra Records took it to the US, UK, Russia, Italy and Spain in May 2018, followed by France, Germany and Australia. It went to No.1 on the Russian and CIS airplay charts, reached No.17 in France and No.36 in Germany, and passed 150 million streams on Spotify. In 2020, Desperados used it in their "We Are the Party" campaign.
