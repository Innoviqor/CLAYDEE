How Bad Do You Want It: Utopia on the Furious 7 soundtrack

Sevyn Streeter's single for Furious 7 is built on a sample of "Utopia", the Bang La Decks track Claydee co-wrote. The soundtrack went to No.1 in the US.

2015

Listen on Spotify Watch on YouTube

"How Bad Do You Want It (Oh Yeah)" by Sevyn Streeter came out on 23 February 2015 as the second single from Furious 7: Original Motion Picture Soundtrack. The song samples Aero Chord's Festival Trap Remix of "Utopia", the single by Bang La Decks, Claydee's masked duo on Ultra Music.

That sample puts Claydee's writing on one of the biggest film soundtracks of the decade. "Utopia" was written by Klejdi Llupa (Claydee) and Theodore Economou, and both are credited for the sample on the album's track listing. The official video, filmed in Abu Dhabi, followed on 4 May 2015.
