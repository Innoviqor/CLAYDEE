Mamacita Buena: where it started

The 2012 summer single that took Claydee to No.1 in Greece and onto Ultra Music worldwide.

2012

Listen on Spotify Watch on YouTube

Claydee released "Mamacita Buena" in the summer of 2012. It went to No.1 on the Greek airplay chart and was picked up by Ultra Music for an international release, one of the first Greek-made dance records to travel that far.

The song later appeared in Telenovela, NBC's comedy series starring Eva Longoria. Its follow-up, "Sexy Papi" in 2013, went to No.1 on airplay in Greece, Poland and Serbia, and Claydee was nominated for Best Greek Act at the MTV EMAs.
